# PROBLEM STATEMENT

## Domain (previously unseen in this account's public surface)

Multiplicative interval systems on the positive reals under group generation by a small set of rational generators. Specifically: the free abelian group generated by the octave (2/1) and the perfect fifth (3/2), projected onto a finite pitch class set, and the residual obstruction that appears when attempting global closure (the Pythagorean comma).

This is not music theory packaging. It is a concrete instance of:
- free generation under multiplicative constraints,
- finite quotient / identification,
- measurement of the resulting cocycle residual,
- decision whether to absorb the residual by tempering or to keep it as an explicit obstruction.

No domain-specific templates from oil/gas, protein, EHD residual, or agent harnesses are reused.

## Required process (hard)

RESTRICT → REPRESENT → CONSTRUCT → MEASURE → RESOLVE → V&V → CERTIFY

The repository is the execution artifact. Intermediate structure, failed hypotheses, numerical measurements, corrections, and negative results must remain visible in the history and in the working tree.

Protected kernel remains fail-closed: any claim of "closed" or "zero residual" that fails numerical verification is rejected.

## Success criterion for the demonstration

An independent observer can reconstruct, from the repository alone, that the same underlying methodology (not a pre-existing domain template) produced the final constrained computational object.
